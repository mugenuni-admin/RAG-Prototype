Pilot Validation: The Ulva Feed Revolution

Theoretical desktop models projected high feed costs based on standard daily rationing. However, data extracted from the 3.5-year commercial pilot proved that a 6% wet biomass ration delivered every 48 hours maximizes growth while drastically cutting feed volume and distribution labor.

Financial Impact of Pilot Data

  • ✗
    Desktop Assumption (Daily Feed) Required 1,440 tonnes of wet feed annually, driving the feed cost to $2.10 per harvested unit.
  • ✓
    Pilot Validated (48-Hour Cycle) Reduces consumption to 383 tonnes of wet feed annually, slashing the feed cost to just $0.59 per unit.
  • ★
    Labor Optimization Halving the feeding frequency directly cuts the physical man-hours required for distribution by 50%.
